[h1]Slay the Spire 2 Balance Mod (Sts2BalanceMod)[/h1]

Aims to improve the balance and gameplay experience of Slay the Spire 2, featuring card reworks, returning classic content from the original game, relic and merchant adjustments, random events, and monster mechanic reworks.

[b]Supported Languages[/b]: Simplified Chinese, English, Italian, Russian
[b]Required Dependency[/b]: Subscribe to and enable [url=https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3747602295]RitsuLib[/url] in the Steam Workshop
[b]Full Documentation & Changes[/b]: [url=https://github.com/MT-SUPER-POWER/STS2-Balance-MOD]GitHub Repository & Full README[/url]

[hr][/hr]
[h2]Mod Overview[/h2]

• [b]Fully Configurable Modules[/b]: Offers 80+ independent toggles in the in-game RitsuLib menu. Every card, relic, event, and mechanic tweak can be individually enabled or disabled. Disabled content is automatically hidden from the compendium and card pool, while remaining fully compatible with existing saves.
• [b]Classic STS1 Content Returns[/b]: Faithfully ports popular cards, classic relics, random events, and iconic elites/bosses from Slay the Spire 1.
• [b]Vanilla Pain Points & Balance Reworks[/b]: Smoother high-ascension card removal prices, reworks for overly restrictive cards, removal of trap cards from the card pool, and rebalanced monster/boss mechanics.
• [b]QoL & Multiplayer Optimizations[/b]: Automatic configuration validation in multiplayer, chest-skipping options, full controller support, and comprehensive localization across all supported languages.
