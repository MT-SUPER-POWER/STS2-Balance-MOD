[h1]Slay the Spire 2 Balance Mod (Sts2BalanceMod)[/h1]
Improves the balance and gameplay experience of Slay the Spire 2 with reworked cards, relics, shops, events, and bosses.

[b]Supported languages[/b]: Simplified Chinese, English, Italian, Russian
[b]Required dependency[/b]: Subscribe to and enable [url=https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3747602295]RitsuLib[/url] in the Steam Workshop
[b]Source code and full documentation[/b]: https://github.com/MT-SUPER-POWER/STS2-Balance-MOD

[hr][/hr]
[h2]Fully Configurable Modules[/h2]
Customize the mod in depth through the RitsuLib menu (86 toggles):
• [b]What You See Is What You Get[/b]: Disabled cards and relics are completely hidden from the Card Library and Relic Library.
• [b]Compatible with Existing Saves[/b]: Disabling content does not remove its underlying model, so existing saves containing it remain playable.
• [b]Restart in Place[/b]: After changing a toggle, a [b]Save and Restart Game[/b] button immediately appears below it.
• [b]Multiplayer Validation[/b]: Multiplayer automatically compares each player's active configuration and clearly identifies any mismatched options.

[hr][/hr]
[h2]Shops and General Rules[/h2]
• [b]High Ascension Card Removal Prices[/b]: At A0–A5, card removal remains 50 gold (+25 each time). At A6+, the base price is [b]75 gold[/b] (+25 each time). Smiling Mask always sets it to 50 gold.
• [b]Skip Chests[/b]: In single-player, holding Cursed Key lets you click “Skip Chest” in a chest room to leave immediately and avoid the curse.

[h2]Card Reworks and Balance Changes[/h2]
• [b]Dirge[/b]: Necrobinder Power. Upgraded version gains [b]Retain[/b].
• [b]Blade Dance[/b]: Silent Skill. No longer [b]Exhausts[/b], promoted to Uncommon, and can be reused.
• [b]Acrobatics[/b]: Silent Skill. Downgraded to Common.
• [b]Biased Cognition[/b]: Defect Power. The Focus-loss cap equals the amount of Focus gained.
• [b]Multicast[/b]: Defect Skill. Its upgrade now adds [b]Retain[/b] instead of the original X+1 effect.
• [b]Wraith Form[/b]: Silent Power. Removes the Dexterity-loss drawback, returning it to the pure Intangible effect from Slay the Spire 1.
• [b]Reprogram[/b]: Defect Skill. Gain 1 Energy and draw 1 card (2 when upgraded).
• [b]Energy Drain[/b]: Necrobinder Attack. Deals 6/8 damage; upgrade all cards in the discard pile.
• [b]Taunt[/b]: Necrobinder Skill. When upgraded, summon with 6 HP and gain 9 Block.
• [b]Wither[/b]: Can be played for 1 Energy and Exhausted; each two Hourglass enhancements increase its cost by 1.
• [b]Radiance[/b]: Immediately draw 2 cards this turn; upgraded version also grants 1 Stardust.
• [b]Bloodletting[/b]: Returned to Common rarity.
• [b]Recycle[/b]: Defect Power. Downgraded from Rare to Uncommon, reducing the threshold for Focus builds.
• [b]Meteor Strike / Heavy Blade[/b]: Meteor Strike's damage scaling is 15/20; Heavy Blade's base damage is increased to 16 (scaling 5/7).
• [b]Well-Laid Plans[/b]: Uncommon, 1 Energy. At end of turn, choose up to 1/2 cards in your hand to retain (stacks and automatically excludes Innate Retain cards).
• [b]Grand Finale[/b]: X-cost AOE. Can be played when draw pile size is ≤ X; upgrade consumes 2 less Energy.
• [b]Coolheaded[/b]: Uncommon, 1 Energy. Whenever you play a Power, draw 1 card (2 when upgraded).
• [b]Fight or Flight[/b]: Standardized the name of Ironclad's “Eager” card across all languages.
• [b]Trap Card Removal[/b]: Removes Synchronize, Synthetic, Sleight of Hand, Precision, and Sidestep from card pools.

[h2]New and Returning Cards[/h2]
• [b]Reaper[/b]: Ironclad, 2-Energy Rare. Deal 4/6 AOE damage and heal for unblocked damage dealt. [b]Exhaust.[/b]
• [b]Second Wind / Evolve[/b]: Ironclad, 1-Energy Uncommon. Second Wind grants 15/20 Block and adds 2 Wounds; Evolve draws 1/2 cards whenever a Status card is drawn.
• [b]Concentrate / Eviscerate[/b]: Silent Uncommons. Concentrate costs 0, discards 3/2 cards, and grants 2 Energy; Eviscerate costs 3 and deals 7/9×3 damage, reduced by discards.
• [b]Setup[/b]: Silent, X-cost Rare. For the next X (X+1 when upgraded) turns, draw 1 extra card and gain 1 extra Energy each turn. [b]Exhaust.[/b]
• [b]Electrodynamics[/b]: Defect, 2-Energy Rare. Channel 2/3 Lightning Orbs; Lightning Orbs hit all enemies.
• [b]Consume / Fission[/b]: Defect Rares. Consume costs 2, loses 1 Orb slot, and gains 2/3 Focus; Fission costs 0, removes all Channeled Orbs (evokes them when upgraded), gains Energy, and draws cards.
• [b]Recursion[/b]: Defect Common. 1 (0 when upgraded) Energy. Evoke your rightmost Orb and channel the Orb that was just Evoked (preserves Dark/Glass Orb accumulated stats).
• [b]Bullseye[/b]: Defect, 1-Energy Uncommon. Deal 8/11 damage and apply 2/3 Lock-On (Orbs deal 50% more damage and prioritize that target).
• [b]Clash / Body Slam[/b]: Necrobinder cards. Clash is a 2-Energy Uncommon that deals 8+7/9 damage and heals the lower-health target; Body Slam is a 2-Energy Common that makes Osty lose HP to deal 20/26 AOE damage.
• [b]Friend Strike[/b]: Ironclad, 1/0-Energy Uncommon (multiplayer only). Deal damage equal to an ally's highest Block.
• [b]Witch Form[/b]: Ancient, 3-Energy Power (obtained from the doll). Each turn, transform cards in hand into upgraded Witch attacks and defenses.

[h2]New and Returning Relics (22)[/h2]
• [b]Inserter[/b]: Defect-exclusive Rare. Gain 1 Channeled Orb slot every 2 turns.
• [b]Blue Candle[/b]: Uncommon. Play otherwise unplayable Curses; lose 1 HP and Exhaust them when played.
• [b]Handcrafted Doll[/b]: Ancient relic (Tanx). At combat start, apply 2 Vulnerable and 2 Weak to all enemies; in exchange, lose 50% max HP and gain [b]Witch Form+[/b].
• [b]Sundial / Orange Pellets / Dead Branch[/b]: Gain 3 Energy every third shuffle / clear debuffs after playing one Attack and one Skill in the same turn / whenever a card Exhausts, add a random card to hand.
• [b]Omamori / Peace Pipe / Smiling Mask[/b]: Negate 2 Curses / add a Peace Pipe card-removal option at rest sites / card removal at merchants always costs 50 gold.
• [b]Coffee Dripper / Fusion Hammer / Cursed Key[/b]: Dwarf Energy relics. Gain +1 Energy; the respective drawbacks are being unable to Rest, Smith, or open chests without gaining a Curse.
• [b]Dwarven Anvil / Soul Contract / Strange Spoon[/b]: Choose 3 cards to permanently reduce their costs / choose 1 card to remove Exhaust (may skip) / playing an Exhaust card has a 50% chance to send it to the discard pile instead.
• [b]Kunai / Hovering Kite[/b]: Silent-exclusive. 0-cost Attacks deal +4 damage / the first time you discard a card each turn, gain +1 Energy.
• [b]Necronomicon / Nilry's Codex / Enchiridion[/b]: Book event relics. Gain Necronomicurse and make your first Attack costing 2+ hit twice / choose a card to upgrade at end of turn / start combat with a 0-cost Power.
• [b]Mutagenic Strength / Mark of the Bloom[/b]: Start combat with +3 Strength and Decay / upgrade all cards, but can no longer heal.

[h2]Vanilla Relic Restorations and Reworks (12)[/h2]
• [b]Sandcastle[/b]: Orobos Ancient. Choose 3 cards to upgrade, then upgrade 3 additional random cards.
• [b]Orichalcum / Toxic Egg / Red Mask[/b]: Retained Block increased to 15 / card removals increased to 4 / removed from the general pool and made event-only.
• [b]Lesson Learned / Cooking Gloves / Royal Seal[/b]: Repeat the previous turn's Attacks or Skills / choose a card to Exhaust (may skip) and gain +1 Strength / allows Powers to receive Enchantments.
• [b]Diamond Crown / Signet Ring / Ornate Bracelet[/b]: Take half damage when playing ≤2 cards / gain 999 gold / choose 4 cards to apply Swift 2 enchantments.
• [b]Neow's Lament / Pandora's Box[/b]: Enemies have 1 HP for the first 3 combats (single-player only) / transforming cards adds an asynchronous confirmation dialog that allows save-scumming.

[h2]Random Events and Classic Slay the Spire 1 Monsters / Bosses Return[/h2]
• [b]Vanilla Event Improvements[/b]: The Weaver's card removal costs 75/150 gold; the Exterminator, Strange Man, and Potion events gain leave options.
• [b]Returning Slay the Spire 1 Events[/b]: Dead Adventurer (loot with increasing encounter chance), Match and Keep (4×3 memory matching), Old Beggar, Cleric, Sacred Spring, Cursed Tome, Masked Bandits, The Joust, Mind Bloom, Wheel of Change, The Library, Tomb of Lord Red Mask, and The Coliseum.
• [b]Monster Reworks[/b]: Eternal Hourglass (Wither can be played and Exhausted) and Infected Prism (a four-turn cycle plus an infection mechanic).
• [b]Classic Slay the Spire 1 Elites[/b]: Lagavulin (sleep and attribute drain), Sentries (multi-target rotation and Plated Armor), and Gremlin Nob.
• [b]Slay the Spire 1 Boss Ports (Acts From The Past)[/b]: Fully ported form changes, two-stage splits, and unique sound effects for The Guardian, Hexaghost, and Slime Boss!
