Thank you for all of your feedback over the past couple of weeks on our BIG beta patch! This beta patch is a mostly Fool-centric one, with lots of reworks, changes, nerfs, buffs, and even a brand new card! There are also a few art additions and tons of bug fixes. We'll be focusing more on polishing up Dread in the future, so keep the feedback coming!

### CONTENT & BALANCE:

**Enemies:**

- Changed **Hard to Kill** power: now only counts HP loss, not damage to Block (meaning this power does not work on blocked damage)

**Colorless Cards:**

- Reworked **My Shinies** card: "Deal 1 damage for every 20(10) Gold you have." → "Deal 2(3) damage. Hits an additional time for every 25 gold you have."

**Fool:**

- The Fool's "What Am I?" Epoch now unlocks Honing instead of Quiver
- Added new card, **Optimism**: Rare - Skill - Cost 1 - "Exhaust ALL Sadness in your Hand. Draw 1 card for each card Exhausted. Exhaust. (Lose Exhaust.)."
- Reworked **Growth** card: "Exhaust ALL Sadness in your Hand. Gain 4(6) Stretch for each card Exhausted." → "Gain 8(11) Block. Transform 1 random Sadness in your hand into a copy of this card."
- Reworked **Arrow Stab** card: Common - Attack - Cost 1 - "Deal 9(12) damage. If you have an Arrow, put a card that can Shoot from your Discard Pile into your Hand." → Common - Attack - Cost 2 - "Deal 13(15) damage. Gain 6(8) Aim."

**General:**

- If you remove or transform all your cards enchanted with Clone, the Clone rest site option no longer shows up

- Hotkeys no longer press buttons on menus that are closed or hidden, and the pause menu keeps blocking hotkeys after you inspect a card or relic from it
- Hotkeys no longer reach the screen behind an open popup or error message
- A tutorial that couldn't appear because another popup was already open now shows later instead of being skipped for good
- Older runs no longer disappear from Run History after playing on another computer or reinstalling Steam

**Enemies:**

- Fixed the impaled arrow on **Test Subject** being very small
- Fixed controller nav breaking if you are hovering over the **Fat Gremlin** when it runs away

- Fixed Fool potion/relic outlines in the potion and relic compendiums

**Events:**

- **Wuppy** no longer reduces **Melancholy** card's cost if it is dead at the start of combat

**Fool:**

- Fool is now included in Bestiary stats
- Fixed **Cho Chorus** not triggering the autoplayed **Wah Blast** double hit effect
- Adroit enchantment on **Bounce** card no longer adds the **Stretch** that Bounce just gave to its Block

- Fixed **Timber** card's AOE damage not being affected by **Gigantification Potion**
- Fixed **Blot out the Sky** card generating no Arrow cards for non-Fool characters

- Fixed an Arrow being lost for the rest of combat when its target died before the Arrow hit
- Fixed **Conductor's Baton** relic including hovertip of its own power
- Fixed Fool's Arrow UI blocking Crusher's power hitbox

**Multiplayer:**

- Fixed Dominion tooltips for Kaiser Crab and Vantom displaying incorrect numbers
- Fixed Fool's **Ensemble** card decrementing multiple times from cards that have replay
- Selecting Random while you don't have it unlocked as the host in multiplayer no longer makes the ascension picker disappear forever
- Fixed hotkeys not working after losing connection to a Blitz or Dread lobby while its info popup was open
